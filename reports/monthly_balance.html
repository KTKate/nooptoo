$10,000 month by month: the strategy vs SPY

Backtest, January 2024 to September 2026, before taxes. Color shows what is held: the overnight strategy (single ML model, net of trading costs) or SPY, a fund of the 500 largest US companies. Line style shows what happens to gains: solid keeps everything invested; dashed moves half of each month's gain to savings. Dashed lines show total money, invested plus saved.
